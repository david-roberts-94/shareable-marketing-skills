## Never do this (AI slop)

- Images that bounce, spin, or wobble for no reason
- Italic serif fonts for captions
- Camera zooms or shakes that do not follow the words
- Fake app screens, fake reviews, or made-up numbers on screen
- Text over the face's eyes and mouth
