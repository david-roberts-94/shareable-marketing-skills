# Hooks

The hook is the words of the first 3 to 5 seconds. A clear hook beats a clever one: a viewer who
knows what they get keeps watching.

## The five questions

Remove each hook that fails one.

1. **Proof:** does it give a real result (a number, a name, a company)?
2. **Promise:** does the viewer know what they get from the video?
3. **Concrete:** is there one number or one name that the viewer can picture?
4. **Fast:** does the promise come before about word 18 (about 6 seconds)?
5. **Stakes:** will the viewer feel they lose something if they stop watching?

## Methods that work

These are methods, not templates. Do not copy the words. Fill the method with the new material.

| Method | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| The result first | Someone got a big, specific result, and here is how | "This agency got 1,000 B2B leads from one simple AI LinkedIn system." |
| The big number about a name | A company and a surprising number, then "here's how" | "This company makes 700 video ads a month with just 50 creators. Here's how." |
| The list with a payoff | A number of things, and what they do for you | "These five websites will rank your business on Google faster than your own site." |
| The free version | Others charge for it; here it is free | "People sell this setup for $90. Here's every step for free." |
| The direct command | The video opens on the instruction itself | "Claude, add all 20 of these effects to this video." |
| The gap | What people type versus what they think they type | "Nobody searches 'AI note taker'. They type a whole sentence." |
| The proof on screen | The video itself is the proof | "This Reel was edited by AI, and so were my last 18." |
| The enemy | A common belief or habit that costs the viewer | "Stop paying for editing courses." |

## Hooks from other subjects

Borrow the method, not the words:

- **Food:** "This is the recipe behind…" (a secret made simple)
- **Fitness:** "Most people quit after…" (a common failure, then the fix)
- **A story:** "Three months ago I…" (only when it is true for the creator)

## Common mistakes

- The promise hides behind a question ("Want to know the secret…?").
- Two numbers fight in one sentence.
- The hook is about the creator, not about what the viewer gets.
- Claimed proof that the creator never gave you.
