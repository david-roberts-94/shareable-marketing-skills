# Prompt 4: find ideas with Klint AI

A post that got double its creator's normal views is proof that the idea works, not luck of a big
account. That is the filter here.

```text
Use Klint AI to find Instagram Reels about [YOUR NICHE] from the last 30 days.
Keep only Reels that got at least 2x their creator's normal views (compare each Reel with the
median views of that creator's latest 30 Reels).

For each Reel give me:
- the link and the creator
- its views, and the creator's normal views
- the hook: the first sentence, word for word
- what the video teaches or shows, in one line
- why it worked
- how I could make my own version of it

Give me the 10 best, ranked by how far they beat the creator's normal views.
Add them to ideas.md in my video-factory folder, newest search on top.
```

## What you should see

Ten idea cards in `ideas.md`, each with proof (the views compared with the creator's normal
views). A Klint search takes about 3 to 10 minutes and uses credits. New accounts get free
credits at https://tryklint.ai.

## Make it better

- Run it again with other words for your niche ("Claude Code", "AI ads", "real estate marketing").
- Ask: "Do the same for TikTok" or "for YouTube Shorts".
- When you pick an idea, say: "Use the video-script skill on idea 3."
