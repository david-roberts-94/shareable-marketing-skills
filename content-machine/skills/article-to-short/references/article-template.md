# The experiment article template

Every article in the machine is a real experiment, written up by the person who ran it. Both
skills in this pack read this file.

## The rules

1. **Real results only.** The creator runs the experiment first and gets the result. Then they
   write. If the result is not real, do not publish the article. Readers and the algorithm both
   punish fake results.
2. **The outcome is in the title.** Readers want the result, not the problem or the tool.
3. **Ride a trend.** Use the newest launch or topic that your audience is searching for this week.
4. **Teach the whole method.** Every step gets the exact prompt, setting, or screenshot, so that a
   reader can do it the same day.
5. **Your product comes last.** It shows up once, in the last step, where it really fits. The rest
   of the article is useful without it.

## The title

```text
I [did a specific thing] with [this week's trend]. It [got a specific result].
```

Examples of the shape:

- "I taught OpenAI how to market my app. It generated 5.4 million views."
- "We let Muse run our TikTok for 24 hours. It generated 1.1 million views."

## The structure

```text
1. The result, in the first two lines
   The number, the time it took, and what it was for.

2. The setup
   What the creator used (tools, accounts, budget, time) and why this trend.

3. The steps (usually 4 to 8)
   ## Step N: [a verb + the thing]
   - What to do, in plain words
   - The exact prompt, in a code block
   - A screenshot of the result
   - One mistake to avoid

4. What still stays manual
   The parts a human must still check, and why.

5. The last step: [your product]
   One short section: how your product does the last part of the job.
   Example: "Step 6: Schedule every slideshow in Postiz."

6. The numbers again
   A small table of the results, with links to the real posts.
```

## Before it goes live

- [ ] Every number has proof: a screenshot or a link to the live post.
- [ ] Every prompt is in a code block, word for word.
- [ ] A reader who has never done this could follow it.
- [ ] Your product appears in one section only.
- [ ] The creator approved the final text.
