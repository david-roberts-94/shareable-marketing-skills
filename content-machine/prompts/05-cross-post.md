# 05 · Post the Short everywhere and send the DMs

After the `article-to-short` skill writes the script, and you make the video in HeyGen, post it
to every short-form platform with the same comment word.

```text
Here is a short video and its files: [video file], caption.txt, dm.txt, and the comment word [WORD].

Make a posting plan for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels:
1. For each platform: the caption (fit it to the platform's length limit, keep "Comment [WORD]" in the first two lines), the hashtags (3 to 5), and the best time to post for [your audience and time zone].
2. The DM that each commenter gets: dm.txt, with the article link [link].
3. The setup steps for the comment-to-DM automation: in ManyChat for Instagram and Facebook, and the manual steps for YouTube and TikTok, where I reply to comments myself.

Give it as a checklist I can follow in 10 minutes.
```

**What you get:** a 10-minute posting checklist. Use any scheduler you like to post all four at
once.
