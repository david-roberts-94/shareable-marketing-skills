# 03 · Turn the creator's notes into a draft

The creator ran the experiment. Now they give Claude their notes, prompts, screenshots, and
numbers. Paste this with `templates/article-template.md` attached.

```text
You are helping me write an X article about an experiment I ran. Use the attached template exactly: the title formula, the structure, and the rules.

Here is everything from the experiment:
- The trend: [the launch or topic]
- The goal: [what I wanted]
- The result: [the real numbers, with dates]
- What I did, in order: [paste notes]
- Every prompt I used, word for word: [paste]
- The tools: [list]
- Screenshots: [attached]

Write the draft. Keep my own words where you can. Put every prompt in a code block, word for word. Mark each place where a screenshot goes with [SCREENSHOT: what it shows]. Mention [product] only in the last step.

Then list any claim in the draft that has no proof in my notes, so I can add the proof or cut it.
```

**What you get:** a draft that the creator edits and approves. Never publish a number that the
creator can't prove.
