# 01 · The weekly trend check

Run this every Monday in Claude with Klint AI connected. It picks the trends for this week's
experiment articles.

```text
I sell [your product: one line on what it does] to [your buyers].

Use Klint AI to look at X, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Reddit for the last 7 days. Find:

1. The 10 newest launches or topics in [your niche, for example "AI tools for marketing"] that people are posting about the most right now. For each one, give 2 real posts with their views and links.
2. For each topic, how a creator could use it in a real experiment that gets a result my buyers want (for example: views, leads, sales, hours saved). Write each as an article title: "I [did X] with [topic]. It [got result]."
3. Rank the 10 ideas by how fast the topic is growing and how close it is to what my product does.

Show the top 5 in a table: topic, proof posts, experiment, title, why it fits.
```

**What you get:** 5 experiment ideas, each tied to a trend with real posts behind it. Send the
best one or two to your creators with the brief (`templates/creator-brief.md`).
