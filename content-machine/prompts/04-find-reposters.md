# 04 · Find the reposters

Every article gets a guaranteed push from a group of paid reposters. Their followers must be your
buyers, not just a big number. Run this in Claude with Klint AI connected.

```text
Here is an X article I want to promote: [link or paste].

My buyers are [who they are]. Use Klint AI to find 30 X accounts whose followers are those buyers. Look at who posts about [topics], who gets replies from [buyer titles or niches], and who reposts content like this article.

For each account, give: handle, followers, median views of their last 20 posts, how often they post, whether they have done paid reposts before (a "Paid partnership" label or "#ad"), and one recent post that shows their audience.

Leave out accounts with views far below their follower count, and accounts with no posts in the last 14 days.

Rank them by median views, and show the top 15. Then write a short, personal DM for each one (under 60 words) that offers a paid repost or quote post of this article.
```

**What you get:** a ranked list and a DM for each account. Log everything in
`templates/tracker.csv`. Every paid repost uses X's paid-partnership label.
