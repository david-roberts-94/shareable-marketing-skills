# 02 · Find the creators who write the articles

The writers must already get real views in your niche. Run this in Claude with Klint AI connected.

```text
Use Klint AI to find 20 creators on X who post about [your niche] and get real results that they show in public (screenshots of views, revenue, or growth).

For each creator, give: handle, followers, median views of their last 20 posts, one post where they show a real result, and whether they have written X articles before.

Leave out accounts that sell courses with no proof, accounts with no posts in the last 30 days, and accounts where most posts are giveaways or engagement bait.

Rank them by median views compared to their followers. Show the top 10 in a table.
```

**What you get:** a short list of writers with proof. Pay matters less than fit: one writer who
really gets views is worth more than five who don't.
